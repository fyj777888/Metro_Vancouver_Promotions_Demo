What matters most to you?

Tell us what should matter more when we find promotions for you. Shopping interests favours the categories you enjoy. Promotion location favours offers in your chosen area. The other three choices help you discover finds shared by people with a similar household income, people from your area, or people with the same gender selection. Choose High to give a factor more influence, Default to keep its usual influence, or Low to give it less. For example, if nearby offers matter most, set Promotion location to High. If you prefer finds from neighbours, set People from your area to High. Low uses half the default weight and High uses one and a half times the weight. A factor affects a recommendation only when that part matches your profile. Promotions that fit your priorities better appear first. The percentages show the relative share of your chosen priorities. These settings change recommendation order; your filters decide which promotions appear. Your income range is not displayed on your promotions.
